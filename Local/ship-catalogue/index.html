        Cargo & fitting space

        Factory systems are already included in the base layout. Kinetic and ordnance services, empty magazine hardware, shield generators and sensors reserve hold space per ship. Carried ammunition takes additional space: 15 kg per regular round, 80 kg per missile and 350 kg per torpedo. Lasers are energy based and use no cargo space. Armor, external engine and tank fittings use no hold space. Cargo expansion increases the gross hold.

        Fittings and ammunition reserve weight and volume. Each trade lot contains 250 kg net material; its packaging adds tare and packed volume. The number of lots that fits depends on the good and both remaining hold limits. Removing ammunition frees its space. Torpedo and railgun services take more space than lighter weapon systems.
